15-Yard Specifications

15 Cubic Yard Dumpster • Dimensions

Size & Capacity

  • • Exterior: 16 feet long × 6'11" wide (top) × 4 feet tall
  • • Reinforced steel construction with rear swing door and rollers
  • • Capacity: 15 cubic yards (~4–5 full-size pickup truck loads or 75–100 trash bags)
  • • Rear walk-in / swing door for easy loading
  • • Fits most residential driveways

Mahoning Valley Coverage

Free delivery within 30 miles of Lordstown, Ohio (covers Trumbull & Mahoning Counties including Lordstown, Warren, Niles, Youngstown, Austintown, Boardman, Canfield, and surrounding areas). Outside this range? Call for a quote.

Accepted Materials

  • ✓ Household junk, furniture, appliances (emptied & doors removed)
  • ✓ Construction debris, drywall, wood, roofing shingles
  • ✓ Yard waste, carpet, flooring, general remodeling debris
  • ✓ Cardboard, paper, and mixed non-hazardous waste

Pre-approval required: Concrete, dirt, rock, asphalt, heavy masonry.

Prohibited Materials

  • ✗ Liquids (oil, antifreeze, wet paint — paint must be dried)
  • ✗ Propane tanks, tires, batteries, electronic devices
  • ✗ Hazardous materials
  • ✗ Devices with freon (must have freon removed and tagged by a licensed dealer)

Placement Guidelines

  • • Minimum clear space: ~20' length × 10' width (includes door swing)
  • • Level, hard surface preferred (driveway or pad)
  • • 23' overhead clearance for the truck (no low wires or branches)
  • • Optional driveway protection boards available (+$20)

How to Prepare the Site

  • • Mark preferred placement or note it in your booking
  • • Move vehicles and clear obstacles
  • • Provide gate codes or access instructions
  • • Keep the area clear on delivery and pickup days

Standard Rental Policies

  • Weight: Package includes listed tonnage. $95/ton prorated overage from certified scale ticket.
  • Delivery window: Standard 8:00 AM – 1:00 PM. Same/next-day available based on fleet (typically noon cutoff for next-day).
  • If you’re not home: Driver places per your notes or best judgment and we send a photo confirmation.
  • Extra days: $15/day (max 14 additional). Call to extend.
  • Loading: Debris must remain level with or below the top rim (DOT rules).
  • Cancellation / Reschedule: Free up to 24 hours before scheduled drop. Call (330) 333-5414.
  • Payment: Invoice must be paid in full prior to delivery. We keep payment information on file to cover any overages. Credit/debit card, cash, or ACH for contractors. HERO20 discount applied at checkout (Active & Retired First Responders and Active Duty Military or Veterans only).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I put concrete or dirt in the dumpster?

Heavy materials require pre-approval. Call us first so we can quote the proper rate and ensure safe handling.

What if it rains?

No extra charge. The dumpster is open-top; cover sensitive items if needed.

Do you take appliances?

Yes — emptied, doors removed, and no freon units unless certified empty.

How full can I load it?

Level with or below the top rim. Overfilled bins cannot be transported safely.

How do I get the Hero Discount?

Enter code HERO20 at checkout for $20 off. Valid only for Active & Retired First Responders and Active Duty Military or Veterans.
